**Subject:** Follow-up: using StoryWeaver stories in the Storytime app

Hello [Name / Pratham Books team],

I'm following up on my earlier message from [date] about using StoryWeaver content in Storytime, a children's story-reading app for ages 2 to 12 with a strong audience in Nigeria and across Africa.

We'd like to include StoryWeaver stories under their CC BY 4.0 licence, with full credit. Before we build anything, we'd like to confirm a few points with you directly:

1. **Bulk access.** Is there a data dump, API or other approved way to get stories in bulk, so we download each one once and don't repeatedly crawl your site?
2. **Our intended use.** Please confirm that the licence covers all of the following, including in a paid app:
   - Adapting the text, for example splitting it into short pages and adjusting reading level, including with the help of AI tools.
   - Reading the stories aloud with synthetic and human voices.
   - Using the original illustrations, and, if we ever commission new ones, creating AI-assisted illustrations.
3. **Terms of use.** Do your terms of use have any restriction on automated access or AI use that we should know about?
4. **Per-item licences.** Some stories or illustrations may carry a different licence from CC BY 4.0, such as donor-supplied art. Is there a reliable way to tell which items are fully CC BY 4.0, for example a metadata field?
5. **Credit wording.** What exact credit would you like us to show: author, illustrator, translator, publisher and donor? Should we link back to the story page? We will state when a story has been adapted.

We'll show the credits on each story and keep a record of where every story came from. We'd also be glad to talk about collaborating, for example sharing which stories children read most in Nigeria.

Thank you for your time. I'm happy to jump on a call.
